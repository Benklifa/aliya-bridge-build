ITEM 1 — WHAT INVESTMENT SERVICES AND ADVICE CAN YOU PROVIDE ME?

We offer personalized investment advisory and financial planning services to retail investors, with a specialization in U.S.-Israel cross-border financial planning for individuals and families making Aliyah (relocating to Israel).

Our principal services include:

  • Portfolio Management: Professional portfolio management using tax-aware investment strategies that consider dual-country (U.S. and Israel) tax implications. We construct and monitor customized investment portfolios using equities, bonds, ETFs, and mutual funds.
  • Financial Planning: Comprehensive financial planning addressing retirement, estate, currency conversion, cross-border tax strategy, and Aliyah readiness.
  • Investment Consulting: Guidance on executive equity compensation, stock options, and retirement plan allocations.

Monitoring

As part of our standard investment advisory services, we monitor client portfolios on an ongoing basis and provide periodic reviews. We will contact you at least annually to review your financial situation and investment objectives.

Investment Authority

We manage accounts on a non-discretionary basis, meaning we will provide investment recommendations but will always seek your approval before buying or selling investments in your account. You retain full control over all investment decisions.

Account Minimums

We do not impose a minimum account size, though we reserve the right to decline clients where the account size makes it impractical to provide our services effectively.

Other Costs You May Pay

In addition to our advisory fee, you may pay other fees and costs related to your investments, including:

  • Custodian fees: Fees charged by the broker-dealer or custodian holding your assets (e.g., Interactive Brokers).
  • Transaction costs: Commissions or markups/markdowns when buying or selling securities.
  • Fund expenses: Mutual fund and ETF internal management fees (expense ratios).

You will pay fees and costs whether or not you make money on your investments. Fees and costs will reduce any amount of money you make on your investments over time. Please make sure you understand what fees and costs you are paying.

ITEM 3 — WHAT ARE YOUR LEGAL OBLIGATIONS TO ME? HOW ELSE DOES YOUR FIRM MAKE MONEY AND WHAT CONFLICTS OF INTEREST DO YOU HAVE?

Standard of Conduct

When we act as your investment adviser, we are held to a fiduciary standard. This means we are required to act in your best interest and not place our interests ahead of yours. At the same time, the way we make money creates some conflicts with your interests. You should understand and ask us about these conflicts because they can affect the investment advice we provide you.

How We Make Money & Conflicts of Interest

  • AUM-based fees: Our advisory fee is based on the value of assets we manage for you. This creates an incentive to encourage you to increase assets under our management.
  • Insurance commissions: We may receive commissions if we recommend and you purchase insurance products. This creates a potential conflict as we may earn more compensation for recommending certain products.
  • Referral arrangements: We may have arrangements with other professionals (CPAs, attorneys) where we refer clients to one another. These arrangements will be disclosed to you in writing.
